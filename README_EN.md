### Introduction

Surfshark is a Netherlands-based VPN run by **Surfshark B.V.** in Amsterdam, part of the Nord Security group since 2022. Its headline feature is **unlimited simultaneous devices** on a single subscription — no other major provider matches that — alongside **4,500+ RAM-only servers in 100 countries** and a suite that bundles antivirus, data removal and identity tools into one plan.

### How to install and sign in

1. Download the Surfshark app for your platform from the table above.
2. Buy a plan or start the free trial, then confirm your account by email.
3. Open the app, sign in, and press **Quick-connect** for the fastest server, or pick a country from the list.
4. Optionally turn on CleanWeb and the kill switch, or enable NoBorders if your network restricts VPN traffic.

Because connections are unlimited, one account covers every device in a household without juggling logins.

### Server network

Surfshark runs **4,500+ servers across 100 countries** — the widest country spread after Proton — and the entire fleet is **RAM-only (diskless)**, so every reboot wipes the machine and nothing can persist on disk. Location coverage is roughly 46 European, 27 Asia-Pacific, 18 American and 9 Middle East and Africa sites. **MultiHop** routes through two countries at once, and **Dynamic MultiHop** lets you choose both ends yourself. **Everlink**, a patented infrastructure feature, moves you to a fresh server mid-session if one goes down for maintenance, instead of dropping the connection.

### Features

* **MultiHop** — routes through two countries at once; Dynamic MultiHop lets you pick both ends
* **NoBorders** — detects restricted networks and switches to servers that work under censorship, activating automatically
* **CleanWeb** — blocks ads, trackers, malware and cookie pop-ups
* **Bypasser** — split tunneling, with app and URL-level rules including reverse mode
* **Kill switch**, Rotating IP, Dedicated IP and Static IP options, plus **Everlink** session stability

Protocols: **Dausos** (Surfshark's own protocol with post-quantum cryptography), WireGuard, OpenVPN and IKEv2 — the last no longer shipping in the Windows app. Encryption is AES-256-GCM, ChaCha20 and AEGIS-256X2.

### Speed, streaming and torrenting

WireGuard and Dausos are the fast options, and Surfshark consistently places among the quickest providers in independent testing, comfortably clearing 4K playback and large downloads. Streaming works on standard servers rather than dedicated ones: reviewers report reliable access to Netflix libraries in the US, UK, Japan and elsewhere, plus BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Hulu and Prime Video. Every server allows **P2P traffic**, and torrent clients are routed automatically, though Surfshark does not offer port forwarding.

### Privacy and audits

Surfshark is registered in the Netherlands, an EU jurisdiction inside the Nine Eyes alliance — a weaker starting point than Switzerland, which the company offsets with its diskless fleet and audit record. **Deloitte** verified the no-logs policy in 2023 and again in 2025, and **Cure53** audited the infrastructure and the new Dausos protocol in 2026, alongside earlier audits of the browser extensions. Surfshark publishes a warrant canary and its findings at [surfshark.com/trust-center](https://surfshark.com/trust-center). Note that only parts of the codebase are open source, unlike fully open-source rivals.

### Surfshark in China

If Surfshark will not connect behind the Great Firewall, enable **NoBorders** mode — it detects restricted networks, switches to a curated server list and often turns itself on automatically — and connect through Hong Kong, Japan or Singapore. Surfshark does not publish a reliability guarantee for mainland China, and independent 2026 testing finds it works only intermittently there, with blocks tightening around sensitive dates. Set up and test the app before you travel, and keep a backup. Guidance: [surfshark.com/blog/vpn-china](https://surfshark.com/blog/vpn-china).
